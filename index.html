Interactive Method

Turn Fig. 2 into a guided story.

Instead of pasting the method figure and asking the reviewer to decode it, the webpage can reveal the mechanism step by step. Click the stages below.

Stage 01
Retrieve visual memory

Find similar wafer maps and use them as candidate-conditioned visual references.

Stage 02
Collect VLM evidence

Use the target and round-specific references to obtain a new observation over candidate joint states.

Stage 03
Accumulate decision evidence

Fuse VLM and memory evidence rather than committing to a single response.

Stage 04
Adaptive stopping

Stop when the evidence margin between the top two states crosses the decision boundary.
