Psychology-Inspired Diffusion Decision Modeling for Vision-Language Wafer Defect Recognition. DDM-VLM treats VLM responses as noisy observations, retrieves similar wafers as visual-memory cues, and accumulates evidence over valid joint defect states until the top-two margin reaches a decision boundary.

At a glance

From one-shot recognition
to sequential decision making.

Mixed-defect wafer recognition requires predicting a complete multi-hot joint state rather than one isolated class. DDM-VLM uses retrieval as visual-memory conditioning, VLM output as perceptual evidence, and a multi-alternative accumulator as the final decision mechanism.

Method

The complete framework,
using your real Fig. 2.

CLIP ViT-B/32 and FAISS retrieve Top-10 similar wafers. Their unique labels define the VLM candidate space; the retrieved examples are split into three observation rounds (4, 3, 3). VLM perceptual evidence and retrieval-memory evidence are then accumulated over the full valid joint-state space.
